# Website Structure

- `/` Home
- `/inventory` Inventory
- `/inventory/[slug]` Vehicle details
- `/imports` Import from Japan
- `/finance` Finance assistance
- `/sell-your-vehicle` Sell or trade in a vehicle
- `/about` About Shoondili
- `/testimonials` Verified customer stories
- `/faq` Frequently asked questions
- `/contact` Contact and enquiry
- `/brand` Public brand page
- `/privacy` Privacy policy
- `/terms` Terms and conditions
- `/sitemap` Human-readable sitemap

## Home sections

Hero, Featured Vehicles, Why Shoondili, Import Process, Finance Guidance, Latest Arrivals, Trust and Support, verified Testimonials or truthful empty state, FAQ, CTA, Footer.

## Inventory filters

Make, model, year, fuel, transmission, mileage, body type, price, monthly estimate, availability, and colour.
