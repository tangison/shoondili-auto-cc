# Background Generation Prompts

These prompts produced the supporting photography in this starter pack. They are for non-vehicle atmosphere only.

## Audi RS7 motion hero

Hyper-realistic cinematic tracking photograph of a black Audi RS7 travelling very fast along a dark Namibian coastal highway at blue hour, distant low three-quarter side/rear view, directional motion blur, wide 16:9, car small in the right third, expansive copy-safe area on the left, muted port lights, near-black and desaturated blue-grey palette. One car only, no visible occupants, no readable plate, text, dealership building, crowds, other vehicles, or watermark. This is approved atmospheric artwork and must not be listed as inventory.

## Coastal road hero

Hyper-realistic cinematic photograph of a dark Namibian coastal highway at blue hour, empty asphalt, one clean road marking leading into the distance, faint warm city or port lights far away, wide 16:9, road concentrated in the right half, generous dark negative space on the left, near-black and muted gold palette. No cars, people, logos, signs, dealership buildings, text, CGI, or watermark.

## Walvis Bay port

Hyper-realistic documentary photograph of Walvis Bay-style port logistics at dusk, stacked shipping containers and one crane silhouette with soft harbour lights, wide 16:9, strongest detail on the left and centre, dark copy-safe area on the right, practical cinematic lighting. No vehicles, people, readable container logos, text, futuristic styling, or watermark.

## Document handover

Hyper-realistic close documentary photograph of a Black Namibian woman and a customer exchanging a black vehicle-document folder across a simple dark table, faces outside the frame, only hands and cropped torsos visible, wide 3:2, action on the right, dark negative space on the left, natural directional light. No readable documents, cars, offices, staged handshake, logo, text, or watermark.

## Key handover

Hyper-realistic macro photograph of a hand holding a simple generic black car key fob above dark textured asphalt, soft warm reflection, wide 3:2, hand and key on the lower left, dark copy-safe area across the upper and right areas. No car, logo, face, showroom, floating key, text, CGI, or watermark.

## Import documents desk

Hyper-realistic top-down photograph of a matte black desk with a closed import-document folder, one metal pen, a small corner of an unreadable shipping form, and soft directional golden light, wide 16:9, objects limited to the lower-right quarter, expansive dark copy-safe area. No cars, people, logos, readable text, office scene, clutter, CGI, or watermark.
